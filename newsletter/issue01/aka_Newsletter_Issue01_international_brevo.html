Fifteen years of a.k.a., and the South African filming season is open.

In 2011 I started a.k.a. in Dubai as a post-production company. Production came later, once we had the right people to run it.

I can’t imagine doing anything else, and I can’t imagine having taken a different route.

Since then we’ve shot commercials across the Gulf and beyond, serviced international features and series, and built a team that now works from Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Riyadh, Cape Town and London.

None of it happens without people. The crews, our post team, the directors who trust us with their work, and the clients who keep coming back. Thank you.

The next fifteen? More films and series alongside the commercial work, and more co-productions between our five cities.

Yasser Obeid
Founder and Executive Producer

 
 

As winter sets in across Europe and North America, South Africa’s filming season is starting, and our Cape Town office is open for business.

From Cape Town we can put a production on the Atlantic coast in the morning and in the winelands by afternoon, with mountain passes, beaches, semi-desert and city streets close by. Johannesburg and the game reserves are a short flight away. Add experienced local crews and good rates, and it’s a strong place to shoot while the north is grey.

We handle service production for commercials, films and series, and work with our London office on international co-productions. If you’re weighing up the numbers, ask us about South Africa’s 25% production rebate and how it works in practice right now.

Talk to Pieter Oosthuizen, our South African producer: pieter@aka.productions

 

This October, a.k.a. set up its UK company, based in London. It’s our production and co-production office, and our base for working on international films and series with UK and European partners.

It also joins two of our bases. The UK and South Africa have an official co-production treaty for film, and a television agreement was added in 2020. a.k.a. now has companies registered in both London and Cape Town, so we can work from either side.

Producers looking for a partner in the UK, the Gulf or South Africa can reach us at hello@aka.productions.

With the South African season now open, Pieter is the person to call for anything from a Cape Town location scout to a full service production: pieter@aka.productions

 

The briefs are coming back to Dubai this autumn, and more of them have an AI line in them.

In the past few months we’ve quoted a two-year, AI-assisted library of performers for an airline. We’ve built key visuals where the car is shot for real and the world around it is created afterwards. And we’re finishing a stills campaign for an automotive client the same way: car in the studio, settings built in post.

None of it feels new to me. I spent more than a decade on Flame, Inferno and Smoke and supervising VFX before I produced anything, and a.k.a. started life as a post house. The tools change. The questions don’t: what has to be real, what can be built, and what holds up on a big screen.

The tools are the easy half. The harder half is the paperwork.

Who owns a performer’s likeness, for how long, and in which markets. That’s the line that surprises people in a budget. If you have a brief with an AI line in it, we’re happy to talk it through: hello@aka.productions
